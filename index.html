A Verified Record — Not a Rumor Tracker

Documented Cases Map

This project tracks suspicious or racially-motivated deaths of Black individuals — including hangings and other violent methods — each entry sourced to law enforcement statements, medical examiner findings, or credible reporting, built to counter unverified viral tallies with a transparent, checkable record.

Every case here is labeled by verification status and method. Nothing is added without a source link. Cases that were misidentified or debunked are kept in the record, not deleted, so the correction itself is visible. Use the Export buttons to pull the underlying data for trend analysis.
